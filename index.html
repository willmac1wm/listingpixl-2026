About

Listing Pixl is a South Jersey real estate media studio helping agents win listings and sell homes faster with photography that stops the scroll.

Questions agents ask

What areas do you cover?

All of Cape May County at the package prices above, from Ocean City and Tuckahoe down to Cape May Point. Outside the county (Somers Point, Millville, Vineland, Buena and beyond) we quote case by case. Send the address and we’ll get back to you.

How fast do I get my photos?

Essential is delivered the next day. Showcase takes 2 days and Premiere 3 days, because they include video editing. Everything arrives edited and MLS-ready.

How are photos delivered?

Through your own private client site on our HD Photo Hub, ready to download for the MLS, Zillow and social. Use Client Login at the top of this page.

Do you shoot drone photos and video?

Yes. Every package includes 10–15 drone photos. Showcase adds a 60-second exterior drone video, and Premiere adds a 1–2 minute cinematic walkthrough.

Do you do twilight photos?

Yes, as virtual twilights: a daytime exterior edited into a glowing dusk shot. Showcase and Premiere include two.

Do you offer 3D tours and floor plans?

A 2D floor plan comes with Showcase, and a Matterport 3D tour with 3 months of hosting comes with Premiere. Ask about adding either on its own.

Do you shoot Airbnb and vacation rentals?

Yes. A vacation rental shoot is $800 for two sessions: a lifestyle shoot and a house shoot.

How do I book?
