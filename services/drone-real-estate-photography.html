Drone Real Estate Photography in Cape May County, NJ

Why listings need aerials

A ground photo shows a house. A drone photo shows the property: where the lot ends, how big the yard is, what sits behind the house and what is around it. In Cape May County that last part often matters most. Buyers want to know how far the beach or the bay is, whether the lot backs up to woods, and what the street looks like. An aerial answers all of that in one image.

Aerial coverage for the property

Standard, Marketing, Luxury and Signature include aerial photos; drone photography can also be added to Essential. We plan views that show the home, driveway, yard and surroundings. A top-down view shows the lot and roofline; wider aerials show nearby water or neighborhood context. Flights depend on suitable weather, airspace and conditions at the property.

Edited like the rest of the shoot

Aerials go through the same editing as the interiors: color correction, straightened horizons and skies that match the ground shots, so the drone photos sit naturally in the MLS gallery.
