HDR Real Estate Photography in Cape May County, NJ

What HDR means for a listing

A camera cannot see a bright window and a shaded room at the same time the way your eyes can. Shoot for the room and the windows go white; shoot for the windows and the room goes dark. HDR (high dynamic range) photography solves that by taking several exposures of each angle and blending them, so the room is bright, the colors are true and the view out the window is still there.

How we shoot a house

We work through the home in the order a buyer would walk it: exterior, living spaces, kitchen, bedrooms, baths and outdoor areas. Straight verticals and careful composition show how the rooms connect. Essential includes 25–30 HDR photos, Standard 35–40, Marketing 40–50, Luxury 50–65 and Signature 65–80+. Standard and above also include drone aerials.

Edited and delivered MLS-ready

Every photo is professionally edited and color-corrected to keep finishes and colors true to the home. Essential, Standard and Marketing include next-day delivery. Confirm delivery timing for video and larger packages when booking. Your media is delivered through your private HDPhotoHub client portal.
