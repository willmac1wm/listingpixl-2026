Real Estate Photography in Cape May, NJ: 3 Blueberry Ln S

Shot June 2024 · Listing agent: Savannah McClintock

About this Cape May listing shoot

Listing Pixl provided professional real estate photography for this Cape May, New Jersey property at 3 Blueberry Ln S 08204. A Cape May ranch with a wood stove, garden beds and drone aerials. The shoot was booked by Savannah McClintock to give the listing scroll-stopping photos for the MLS, Zillow, and social media.

3 Blueberry Ln S is a ranch in a wooded Cape May neighborhood with a cast iron wood stove inside and raised garden beds out back. The shoot covered the living room, kitchen, bathroom and a bedroom, then used the drone for a view of the home and garden and a wide shot over the trees.

What we photographed

  • Front exterior and drone view of the home and garden
  • Living room and cast iron wood stove
  • Kitchen, bathroom with walk-in shower and bedroom
  • Raised garden beds and backyard shed
