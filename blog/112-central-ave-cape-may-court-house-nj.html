About this Cape May Court House listing shoot

Listing Pixl provided professional real estate photography for this Cape May Court House, New Jersey property at 112 Central Ave 08210. A yellow ranch home in Cape May Court House with a back deck, fenced yard and drone views out to the marsh, covered inside and out. The shoot was booked by Jason Ruffenach of eXp Realty to give the listing scroll-stopping photos for the MLS, Zillow, and social media.

This yellow ranch home on Central Ave in Cape May Court House sits under big shade trees, with a small front deck, hydrangeas and a fenced backyard. The shoot covered the kitchen with maple cabinets and a gas range, the living room with its wide front window, the dining area that opens onto the deck, a bedroom and two bathrooms. Outside we photographed the back deck, the open lawn and a stone outdoor fireplace under a pergola. The drone showed the lot from above and the tree-covered neighborhood with marshland on the horizon.
