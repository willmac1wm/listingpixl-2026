Homes in North Cape May

North Cape May is the part of Lower Township between Cape May and the Delaware Bay, near the Cape May-Lewes Ferry terminal. Its streets are mostly one-story ranches and cottages on regular lots, many with fenced yards, back decks and sheds, and the bay beaches are close by. Our North Cape May shoots fit that picture: a gray ranch with dark shutters on a corner lot on Scott Ave with an enclosed sunroom and a detached garage, a ranch on Adriatic Ave with red shutters, a fenced yard and a red barn-style shed, and a ranch on Harmony Ln with green shutters, an updated bathroom, a back deck and a fenced brick patio.

Each of these homes had something buyers notice in person and deserve to see online. At Scott Ave it was the enclosed sunroom with windows on three sides and new stainless appliances in the oak kitchen. At Adriatic Ave it was the bright living room, the open dining area beside the kitchen and the large backyard behind the deck. At Harmony Ln it was the rattan pendant lights in the living room, a kids' room with white bunk beds and a brick patio with a picnic table.

What photos sell a North Cape May listing

Ranches can look alike in listing photos, so the job is to show what makes each one different. Usually that is the outdoor space and the location. We shoot the back deck, the patio and the yard from angles that show their real size, then fly the drone for a top-down view of the lot and a wider aerial toward the bay, which tells buyers how close the water is without opening a map. Inside, one-story homes tend to have rooms that open into each other, like a living room that flows into the kitchen; we compose those shots to show how the rooms connect, and use HDR so the room and the view through the windows are both properly exposed. Sunrooms, kids' rooms and freshly painted spaces get their own frames because they help a family picture living there. If the front of a ranch looks flat under a midday sky, a virtual twilight edit gives the listing a stronger cover photo.
