Homes in Cape May Court House

Cape May Court House is the county seat in Middle Township, set back from the barrier islands with more land and newer neighborhoods than the beach towns, and a short drive from Stone Harbor, Avalon and the Wildwoods. You will find ranches on wooded lots, homes with inground pools and townhome communities. Our shoots here include a townhome on St Andrews Dr with a two-story living room, a primary bedroom that opens onto a balcony and a covered patio facing the lawn; a ranch on Lomurno Ln with a fenced inground pool and a detached two-car garage on a big wooded lot; and a long ranch on Cedar Mill Ln with a large kitchen, a built-in office, an enclosed sunroom and a wide-open lot backed by woods and fields.

Inside, Court House homes tend to be roomy. At St Andrews Dr we photographed a white kitchen with a breakfast bar, a dining room with two-tone walls and a primary bathroom with a soaking tub. Lomurno Ln had a bright great room with a vaulted ceiling and an arched window, a maple kitchen with granite counters and a living room with a fireplace and wood mantel. Cedar Mill Ln had a family room with big windows and a primary bedroom with a sitting area.

What photos sell a Cape May Court House listing

Space is the story in Court House, so drone aerials are the core of the shoot. A top-down view shows the house, the pool and the garage at a glance, and a wider aerial over the woods and fields shows the privacy buyers move inland for. On the ground, pools and patios get several angles, including the view from the back door, so buyers see how the outdoor space connects to the house. Townhome communities need a different approach: one unit can look like the next, so we focus on what stands out inside, such as tall windows, fireplaces and balconies, and use an aerial to show the community around it. Big great rooms with vaulted ceilings are where HDR earns its keep, holding detail in tall windows while the room stays evenly lit. The St Andrews Dr listing also got virtual twilight exteriors, with the townhome row glowing at dusk.
