Homes in Vineland

Vineland, in Cumberland County, covers more ground than any other city in New Jersey, and its neighborhoods reflect that: colonials and ranches on generous lots, with room for driveways, garages and fenced yards. Our Vineland shoot on Pixwhey Ct is a good example of what buyers look for here: a two-story colonial with a two-car garage and a wraparound porch, a two-story living room with an arched window, a dining room with big windows, a finished basement family room, a primary bedroom with a vaulted ceiling and a paver patio leading to a large fenced backyard.

Each space in the Pixwhey Ct home got its own frame. The dining room with a chandelier and big windows sits next to the oak kitchen with its pantry. The finished basement family room has recessed lighting, which helps a basement photograph as warm living space rather than storage. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has a vaulted ceiling and the primary bathroom has a double vanity and a corner tub. Out front, the covered porch with Adirondack chairs gave the listing a welcoming second exterior shot.

What photos sell a Vineland listing

Family buyers in Vineland look closely at space: how many rooms, how big the yard, whether there is a finished basement. Good photos answer those questions in order. We shoot the exterior from angles that show the porch and garage together, cover every main living space, and give finished basements extra light so they look as inviting as the main floor. The backyard gets photographed from the patio so buyers see the fence line and the lawn. Then the drone takes three views: the house, driveway and yard together; a top-down of the whole lot; and a wide aerial of the neighborhood. A two-story living room with an arched window makes a great photo only if the window does not blow out, and HDR blending keeps the arch and the view through it intact. Oak kitchens and pantries get careful color correction so the wood looks the way it does in person.
