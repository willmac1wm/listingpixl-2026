Homes in North Wildwood

North Wildwood takes up the north end of Five Mile Beach, with ocean blocks on one side, the back bay and marshes on the other, and the Hereford Inlet Lighthouse near the tip. Homes run from bayfront townhomes and condos to classic shore houses, and New Jersey Ave is lined with shops and businesses. Our North Wildwood work shows both sides of town: a bayfront townhome on Paradise Way with a waterfront patio, balconies facing the marsh and a primary bedroom that opens onto a bay-view deck, and a retail space on New Jersey Ave, where we photographed the sales floor and a wood-lined back room and flew aerials showing the Welcome to North Wildwood water tower and the surrounding blocks.

The Paradise Way townhome shows what a full waterfront shoot covers: a living room with a gas fireplace and coastal decor, a dining room with a chandelier and farmhouse table, a white kitchen with a marble island, a primary bathroom with a glass shower and double vanity, and a covered balcony with rocking chairs facing the bay. Every room with a window toward the water was shot so the view stays part of the picture, not an afterthought.

What photos sell a North Wildwood listing

Water views sell North Wildwood, and they are easy to undersell. A view out a sliding door turns into a white rectangle if the room is exposed for the interior alone. We bracket every shot and blend the exposures so the bay, the marsh and the sky stay visible through the glass while the room stays bright. On balconies and decks we shoot toward the water and, when the light allows, catch the sunset over the bay, like the balcony shot at Paradise Way with its Adirondack chairs. Virtual twilight edits work well on townhome rows, where every window glows against a dusk sky. For commercial listings we photograph the space the way a buyer or tenant would walk it, from the main floor to the storage and back rooms, then add drone aerials that place the building on the street among its neighbors.
