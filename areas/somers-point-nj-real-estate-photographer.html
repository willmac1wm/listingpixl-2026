Real Estate Photographer in Somers Point, NJ

HDR listing photos, drone aerials and virtual twilights for Somers Point listings, edited and delivered MLS-ready. 1 Somers Point shoot and counting.

Homes in Somers Point

Somers Point sits on Great Egg Harbor Bay in Atlantic County, just across the bridge from Ocean City. It mixes waterfront neighborhoods along the bay with classic suburban streets of two-story homes, capes and ranches. Our Somers Point shoot on Euclid Ave was an exterior and aerial job: a two-story home with a garage and lawn, photographed from the front and from the air so the listing had a strong cover photo and a clear view of the property.

An exterior-and-aerial set is also a good first step for agents who want something live while the house is still being prepared. The front exterior can lead the listing on day one, the aerial shows the property from above, and the interiors can follow once the rooms are ready. When we come back for the inside, the edits are matched to the first set so the gallery looks like a single shoot. Exterior-only and partial shoots are quoted per job.

What photos sell a Somers Point listing

Not every listing needs a full interior shoot on day one. Sometimes an agent needs a strong exterior and aerial set quickly, for a coming-soon post or a listing where the location is the main story, and a drone shot plus a clean front exterior does that. For a full Somers Point listing we add HDR interiors: kitchens, living spaces, bedrooms and baths, shot with blended exposures so rooms look bright and the windows hold their view. Near the bay, the drone is the best tool we have, because an aerial can show how close a home sits to the bay in a single frame, where a ground photo only shows the street. A virtual twilight edit turns a daytime front exterior into a dusk cover photo that stands out in search results. Tell us what the listing needs and we will put together the right set.
