Homes in Buena

Buena is a small borough in Atlantic County, surrounded by the farmland and woods of Buena Vista Township, between Vineland and Hammonton. Homes sit on roomy lots, and outdoor space such as decks, yards, garages and gardens is a big part of why people buy here. Our Buena shoot on Catherine Ave is typical of the area: a two-story home with a two-car garage, a living room with a dark accent wall, a kitchen with sage green cabinets, a dining room with a farmhouse table, a bedroom with big windows, and out back a deck overlooking a large yard with picnic tables and a pergola.

The Catherine Ave shoot covered ten photos, and each answered a different question a buyer might have. The front exterior shows the two-car garage. The aerial shows the home in its yard, and the top-down shows the full lot. Inside, the living room with recliners, the kitchen with stainless appliances and the farmhouse dining table show the main floor, while the bedroom with a ceiling fan and the bathroom with a wood vanity cover the private rooms. The deck and the pergola show where summers happen.

What photos sell a Buena listing

When the backyard is the selling point, it needs more than one photo. At Catherine Ave we shot the back deck, the picnic area and the pergola from the ground, then flew the drone for an aerial of the home and yard and a top-down view that shows the whole lot at once. That top-down shot is the one buyers zoom into, because it shows where the yard ends and how much room there is. Inside, color matters. Painted cabinets and accent walls only help a listing if they look the way they do in person, so we color-correct every frame instead of letting a filter shift the greens and grays. Bedrooms with big windows and bathrooms with patterned wallpaper get balanced HDR exposures so they look bright without washing out. If an empty room needs help, virtual staging can show a furnished version. See current add-on pricing on our Packages page.
