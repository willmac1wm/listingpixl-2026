Homes in Villas

Villas sits on the Delaware Bay side of Lower Township, a grid of quiet residential streets running down to the bay beaches. Most of what we photograph here are one-story ranches, bungalows and shore cottages, many with decks, sheds, outdoor showers and detached garages, plus a steady stream of renovations and new construction. We have shot more listings in Villas than anywhere else: renovated ranches on W Wilde Ave, a new two-story build with white oak floors and a marble shower, a raised cottage with a lattice deck on E Atlantic Ave, a cottage with a beamed great room and a backyard pond a few blocks from the bay, and a blue bayfront home sitting on the dunes above the beach on Village Rd.

Inside, Villas homes vary more than the street view suggests. Our photos here include a coral living room with a blue sectional and a bathroom with bold blue wave tile on W New York Ave, a garage converted into a game room and bar on Rutledge Ave, butcher block counters and a farmhouse sink on Jennings Ave, a sunroom family room with coastal decor on Walnut Ave, and a renovated cottage on Alexander Ave with a quartz kitchen and a finished upstairs bonus room.

What photos sell a Villas listing

Two things sell a Villas listing online: the yard and the bay. Buyers want to see the deck, the fire pit, the outdoor shower and how much lawn comes with the house, so we shoot the backyard as carefully as the kitchen and add a top-down drone view of the lot. Then the drone goes up and faces the water. On many Villas streets the Delaware Bay is a short walk away, and an aerial that shows the bay past the rooftops answers the first question most buyers ask. Inside, Villas cottages are often compact, so HDR matters: blended exposures keep the windows from blowing out and let a small living room read bright and open without stretching it. For a ranch with a plain daytime front, a virtual twilight edit gives the listing a cover photo that stands out in a feed of gray-sky shots, like the ones we made for 15 N 11th St, 116 W Wilde Ave and 10 E Atlantic Ave.
