Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Listing Pixl is a real estate photography business serving Cape May County and South Jersey, New Jersey. This page explains what information this website collects and what we do with it — in plain language.

Information you give us

Our booking form opens your own email app with your message addressed to us — we don't store form submissions on any server. When you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to share (your name, email, phone number, and shoot details) so we can reply and schedule your shoot. Email us directly at ListingPixl.Media@gmail.com or call/text (609) 780-7027.

Information collected automatically

We use two analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site:

  • Google Analytics 4 collects pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, and your approximate location. It does not tell us who you are.
  • Meta Pixel records page views, booking-form submissions, and taps on our call, text, and email links. This helps us measure our advertising and show relevant ads to people who visited the site.

Both tools use cookies or similar technologies in your browser.

How we use information

  • To respond to booking inquiries and schedule shoots.
  • To understand which pages and services visitors care about.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with Google and Meta as described above, so their tools can function.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the site still works.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
  • Control ad personalization in your Meta ad settings.

Children

This site is for business clients and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above.
